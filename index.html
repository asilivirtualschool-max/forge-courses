Take them in order

Course 1 first. The AI course assumes you can already name a threshold concept and a misconception, because those are what you will be adapting for and diagnosing against. Taken the other way round, Course 2 becomes a tools workshop.

How the programme runs

What these courses will not do

  • No lesson observation. Both are portfolio-assessed. Nobody watches you teach.
  • Nothing goes to your school as information about your teaching.
  • No examination. Three pieces of your own work per course, on your own material.

A note on your data

Everything you type — progress, answers, portfolio drafts — is stored in your own browser and nowhere else. There is no account and no server. Use the Export portfolio button on each course to download your work when you are ready to submit it.

One rule in Course 2: no student names, no identifiable student work, and no photographed exit tickets go into any AI tool. Aggregate counts and anonymised quotes only.
