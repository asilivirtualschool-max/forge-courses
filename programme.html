The programme

Two certificated courses, twelve hours, one term. What you do, in what order, and what each certificate attests to.

The shape

CourseWhat it makes you able to do
Course 1
6 hours		Teaching at the Threshold Identify the threshold concept in your own subject and defend the choice. Tell a student who is stuck from one who is mid-crossing. Plan the crossing and build the four documents that carry it.
Course 2
6 hours		AI for Teaching Practice Adapt a resource to a different class without losing what made it work. Read a set of responses for the misconception underneath. Research your own practice and write it up.

Order matters. Course 1 first — the AI course assumes you can already name a threshold concept and a misconception.

What each certificate attests to

Teaching at the Threshold — that you submitted a completed planner row and Sequence Map for a unit you teach, a liminality case analysis distinguishing a stuck student from a liminal one, and a reflective statement. Grounded in Meyer & Land, Perkins, Cummins, Erickson and ACER.

AI for Teaching Practice — that you submitted an adapted resource with a written justification of an AI output you rejected, a misconception analysis from an anonymised response set with the hinge question written from it, and an action-research reflection. Mapped to the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers (2024).

Neither certificate attests to quality of teaching, and neither involves lesson observation. Distinction is available on each, awarded for independent judgement rather than compliance — most visibly, on Course 2, in the rejection justification.

Running it alongside a teaching term

WhenWhat runs
Before termCourse 1 in full. Course 2 Module 1 in the week before teaching starts.
Weeks 1–3Course 2 Module 2 — adapting — once you have material you want to change.
Weeks 4–6Course 2 Module 3 — reading the evidence — once there are real response sets to read.
Weeks 7–9Course 2 Module 4 — action research — with a term of notes to work from.
Weeks 10–12Portfolio submission for both courses. Certification.
Why Course 2 is spread across the term. Delivering all four AI modules up front would be easier to timetable and much less useful. Module 3 is about reading real student responses and Module 4 about interrogating your own notes — neither works on hypothetical material. Front-loading is the commonest way this kind of training becomes something teachers enjoyed and never used.

The four classroom documents

Course 1 Module 4 builds these, and Course 2 adapts them. Each answers a question the one above it cannot.

  • Sequence Map — the whole unit on one page. Which lesson carries the crossing.
  • Lesson Plan — one lesson, with the move declared before any activity is chosen.
  • Worksheet — retrieval, worked example, faded completion, independent work.
  • Exit Ticket — two-tier. The answer and the reason. This is where the evidence comes from.
